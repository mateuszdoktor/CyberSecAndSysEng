Questions
1. Observability includes metrics, logs, and traces with correlation; logging alone is just text output.
2. Metrics provide fast health and trend signals for mission systems (latency, errors, saturation).
3. Logs alone lack aggregated trends, timing context, and cross-service correlation.
4. Traces show end-to-end request paths, timing, and dependencies across services.
5. A span is a timed operation within a trace, with attributes and timing.
6. OpenTelemetry correlates via shared trace/span IDs and consistent resource attributes.
7. Observability reveals anomalies, suspicious behavior, and unusual resource patterns.
8. Correlation links signals to reduce MTTR and identify root cause in distributed systems.
